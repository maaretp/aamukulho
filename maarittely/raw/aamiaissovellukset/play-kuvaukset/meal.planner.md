Meal Planner & Macro Tracker
YouMealPlan brings your weekly meal plan, recipes, grocery list and nutrition goals together in one simple app. Plan breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks, import recipes with AI, create shopping lists automatically and track calories and macros — without juggling different apps.

PLAN YOUR WEEK
Build a weekly meal plan in seconds and always know what’s for breakfast, lunch, dinner or a snack. Save your favorite menus, reuse them whenever you want and make meal prep or batch cooking easier.

IMPORT RECIPES WITH AI
Found something you want to cook on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest or a cooking website? Paste a supported link and the AI recipe importer saves the ingredients and instructions for you. Spend less time typing and more time cooking.

KEEP RECIPES ORGANIZED
Create your personal recipe book and keep favorite meals in one place. Add them to future meal plans whenever you want.

TRACK CALORIES AND MACROS
Add calories, carbs, protein, fat, sugar and fiber to your recipes, or let AI estimate them for you. Set daily nutrition goals and use YouMealPlan as a simple calorie counter and macro tracker. You can also describe a meal in plain words and get an estimated nutritional breakdown.

CREATE A SMART GROCERY LIST
Generate a grocery list automatically from the recipes in your weekly plan. Check items off while you shop, share the list with your family and keep every ingredient in one place.

CHECK PROGRESS FROM YOUR HOME SCREEN
Use the nutrition widget to see your daily calories, macros and goals at a glance without opening the app.
